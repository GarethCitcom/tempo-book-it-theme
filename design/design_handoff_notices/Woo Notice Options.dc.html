Turn 1 · WooCommerce notice treatments

Replacing .wc-block-components-notice-banner

Three ways to present Woo notices in the SJP theme. All three read from the same source markup, so you can ship the restyle now and layer a toast or popup on top of it later. Click the buttons under 1b and 1c to fire them.

1a Simple restyle

CSS only, no JavaScript. The banner keeps its place in the page flow, so screen readers and Woo's own focus handling carry on working. Black keyline and tinted fill swap for a white surface card, a status keyline down the left edge, and a glyph disc in the house status colours.

Use for: everything that arrives on page load, which is most of Woo, including validation errors on checkout where the notice must sit next to the form.
1b Popup dialog

The notice is lifted out of the page into a centred dialog with a dimmed backdrop, and the parent has to acknowledge it. Loud by design, so reserve it for things that change what happens next: a released hold, a declined payment, a class that filled up while they were choosing.

1c Toast

Notices slide in over the page, stack when several land together, and time out on their own. Nothing reflows, so the page does not jump under the cursor, which is what makes it the right fit for the AJAX steps in the booking flow.

Recommendation

Ship 1a as the baseline for every notice, then route by intent: toasts (1c) for anything the plugin triggers over AJAX, popup (1b) only for the handful of blocking states. One plugin setting can pick the default channel per tenant, since the markup and the status tokens are shared across all three.
